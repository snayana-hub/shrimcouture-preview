Our story

Woven by hand, kept for generations

Shrimcouture began with a simple frustration: the best handloom in India rarely reaches the people who would treasure it. We work with a small circle of weaving families, pay them directly, and keep our collection small enough that every saree is chosen by hand.

Each piece arrives with the name of the cluster it came from, the weave it belongs to, and instructions for keeping it for the next thirty years.
