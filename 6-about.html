Our story

A small collection, chosen by hand

Shrimcouture works with weaving families in Kanchipuram, Varanasi, Patan, Chanderi and Lucknow. We buy directly, in small runs, and pay before the loom is set. What reaches you is the same cloth those families make for their own weddings.

Shipping & duties

Shipping within India is complimentary and insured, 3-5 business days. International orders travel by DHL Express with tracking - 5-8 business days to the US, UK, EU, Gulf, Singapore and Australia. Duties and local taxes are calculated at checkout, so nothing is collected on delivery.

Returns

Ready-to-wear pieces can be returned within 14 days of delivery, unworn and with tags. Made-to-measure and altered pieces are final sale. Return shipping within India is free; international returns are at cost.

Fabric care

Silk should be dry cleaned only and stored in a cotton or muslin wrap, never plastic. Refold sarees along a different line every few months so the zari does not crack. Chikankari and cotton can be hand washed cold, in the shade.

Contact

Write to hello@shrimcouture.com or message us on WhatsApp. Our studio replies within one working day.
